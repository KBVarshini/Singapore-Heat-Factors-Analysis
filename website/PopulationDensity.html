Population Density

Population density refers to the measure of the number of people living in a specific zone. This is usually expressed as the number of individuals per unit of land area.

As for Singapore, Population Density refers to the distribution of population across different regions or neighborhoods In my case, I am required to calculated the Population Density data for the subzones of Singapore.

In order to accomplish this task, OpenStreetMap and Nominatim API is used. The data source used is from SingStat.
